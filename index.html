Tasting notes

Pale gold, almost translucent.

White pear and baked apple on the nose, a soft honeyed middle, then the lemon-zest snap that Ugni Blanc is known for. The grape skins give a gentle, tender texture. Less sugar than a classic jam: the fruit leads.

Terroir

Provence Verte, as the bee flies.

Carcès sits in the green heart of the Var, between the Argens river, the Cistercian abbey of Le Thoronet and the limestone hills that feed the lake. Clay and limestone soils, cool nights, hot dry summers: the conditions that keep Ugni Blanc bright and lively.
